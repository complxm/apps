Two Seats of Time

Inside

Time shows itself two ways. From inside there is only now: one note sounding, a trace of the one before, nothing else in hand. From outside everything is laid out at once, and nothing moves — there is no now.

Nothing you do from inside reaches the outside. Dragging the score fails. The only way across is the hold: the outside is given, not derived. That is the claim of the paper: the two seats are joined by trust, not logic.

Tap the sounding note to pray. It re-tunes; the score does not change. Hold to trust afterwards and the re-tuned notes are there — the asking was already part of the plan.
